Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Submitting is 100% free. Featured plans are reviewed in a public YouTube video.

Will every plan be featured?

I get a lot of submissions, so I can't feature them all. I pick plans that will teach the most to other property owners.

Can I submit a hand-drawn sketch?

Yes. Just make sure the photo is clear, well-lit and the room sizes are readable.

My house is already built. Can I still submit?

Yes. Renovation plans and existing homes are welcome. Tell me what you want to change.

What if I want a private, full review?

Mention it in the form and my team will reach out about a paid design consultation.
