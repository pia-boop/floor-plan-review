What to send

The more I know, the better the review

  • Your floor plan (every floor, if there's more than one)
  • A north arrow on the plan, so I can read the sun and wind path
  • Setbacks: the distance from each lot line to the building line
  • Lot size and where the property is (city or province only)
  • Who will live there: family size, parents, helpers, pets
  • The one thing about the plan that keeps you up at night
  • Your rough budget, if you're comfortable sharing

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Submitting is completely free. Featured plans are reviewed in a public YouTube video.

Will every plan be featured?

I can't feature them all. I pick the plans that will teach other property owners the most.

Can I submit a hand-drawn sketch?

Yes. Make sure the photo is clear, well lit, and the room sizes are readable. Coffee stains are optional.

My house is already built. Can I still submit?

Yes. Renovation plans and existing homes are welcome. Tell me what you'd like to change.

What if I want a private, full review?

Mention it in the form and my team will reach out about a paid design consultation.
